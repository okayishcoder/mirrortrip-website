Support

Help for Mirror Trip users and app reviewers.

For support requests, privacy questions, deletion or restoration questions, trip reports, blocking questions, and bug reports, contact the Mirror Trip support team.

support@mirrortrips.com

What support can help with

  • account access or account-related questions
  • account deletion and restoration requests during the grace period
  • privacy-related requests
  • questions about published trips, shared links, or mirrored trip copies
  • questions about reporting trips, blocked users, or unblocking someone
  • bug reports or unexpected app behaviour
  • general questions about using Mirror Trip

Best way to contact support

Email support@mirrortrips.com and include enough detail for the team to understand the request.

For account-specific issues, sending the request from your account email address may help the team verify and respond more efficiently.

FAQ

Common support questions

How do I request account deletion?

Account deletion is requested from within the app. The public deletion page explains what happens immediately, during the grace period, and after permanent deletion.

Can a deleted account be restored?

During the 15-day grace period, restoration requests can be sent to support@mirrortrips.com. Restoration is not guaranteed.

Where can I submit a privacy request?

Privacy-related requests can be sent to the same support email. The published privacy policy is available on this site.

What happens when a trip is published or mirrored?

Published trips may be visible through public-trip and shared-link features. If a trip is available to mirror, another user can create a separate editable copy in their own account.

What happens when I report a trip?

You can report trips you believe are inappropriate, unsafe, abusive, misleading, spam, hateful, or against Mirror Trip's rules. Mirror Trip may hide the reported trip from your own experience while it is reviewed.

How does blocking a trip author work?

If you block a trip author while reporting a trip, that person's published trips will no longer appear for you in places such as Explore, Wishlist, and other browsing areas. Blocking only affects your own experience and does not automatically remove that person or their content for everyone else.

Can I unblock someone later?

Yes. Where the app provides the control, you can view and unblock people you have blocked. After unblocking, their content may appear again where allowed by Mirror Trip's visibility and safety rules.

Are public store download links available here?

This site does not claim live store listings unless official store links are published separately.
