What Mirror Trip Does

More than a trip planner. It turns real travel into guides worth sharing.

Start with a rough idea, build the itinerary, live the journey, and keep improving the details as you go. When the trip is done, publish it as a practical guide that can help another traveler plan better.

Plan before you go

Build your route, days, activities, places, costs, travellers, and notes in one organized itinerary before the trip begins.

Capture the real journey

Keep adding photos, memories, map details, practical tips, costs, and what actually happened while the trip unfolds.

Create your travel guide

Turn your completed trip into a structured guide with routes, highlights, places, notes, photos, and useful details for other travelers.

Inspire other travelers

Publish trips so people can discover your journey, save ideas, share inspiration, and use your experience to plan better.

Mirror trips you love

Found a published trip that fits your style? Mirror it into your own editable plan and customize it for your dates, pace, travellers, and preferences.

Why Mirror Trip

Built for travelers who want their journeys to help others.

Most travel apps stop at planning. Mirror Trip keeps going. It helps you plan the trip, travel it in real life, preserve the details, and publish the experience as a guide others can actually use.

Your trip becomes more than a private memory. It becomes your own travel blog-style guide, with the structure, places, photos, costs, and notes another traveler needs to follow your path.
